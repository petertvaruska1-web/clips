ClipForge privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

ClipForge only accesses the YouTube account of its owner, using the permissions needed to upload videos and to read the channel name and the statistics of its own videos.

  • Google user data is used only to upload and manage the owner's own videos.
  • It is never sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Twitch chat is read anonymously; chat usernames are stored only as one-way hashes.

ClipForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
